How to use Volume Shader BM

The benchmark is intentionally simple: choose a workload, start the timer and inspect the measured result.

Choose the right level

Simple

Use when a device struggles with heavier workloads.

Standard ★

Recommended baseline for most comparisons.

Advanced

Higher shader cost for stronger GPUs.

Extreme

Very heavy workload; use with caution on phones and older PCs.

Choose the duration

Use 30 seconds for a quick comparison, 3 minutes for sustained behavior, or manual mode when experimenting.

Read the result

MetricWhat it tells you
Average FPSOverall frame throughput.
1% lowSlow-frame behavior.
Min / MaxObserved range and outliers.
StabilityFrame-time consistency for this benchmark.

Save and share

The result panel can keep local history in your browser and export a JSON result. A result link encodes the selected test settings so you can share the configuration, but it does not turn the benchmark into a server-side ranking database.
