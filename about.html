About Volume Shader

This is a free site that explains the volume shader GPU benchmark and lets you run a small version in your browser.

What this site is

An independent guide and tool for testing graphics performance. The benchmark here is a lightweight ray-marched Mandelbulb shader, built for quick tests on desktop and mobile.

What it is not

It is not the original VSBM page and is not affiliated with its author. The original lives at cznull.github.io/vsbm. Scores from different benchmark pages should not be compared with each other.

Accuracy

Browser results depend on your browser, driver, power mode and temperature. We describe them as indicators, not exact hardware ratings.
