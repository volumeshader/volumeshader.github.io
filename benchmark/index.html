Standardized Testing Protocol & Repeatability Rules

To ensure your VolumeShader BM score is fair and comparable with other devices on the Hardware Comparison Database, follow these testing conditions:

1. Close Background Tabs

Video streams, background tabs, and browser extensions consume compositor threads and introduce false frame drops.

2. Connect AC Power

Laptops and mobile devices throttle GPU clock speeds by 30% to 50% when running on battery power to conserve energy.

3. Cold Device Run

Allow mobile devices and ultrabooks to cool down for 2 minutes prior to running the test to measure peak baseline performance.
