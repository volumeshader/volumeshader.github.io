What a graphics test can tell you

Frame rate

How many frames the browser is producing under this workload.

Slow frames

Whether the lowest part of the run is much weaker than the average.

Consistency

A simple stability score shows frame-time variation over the measured sample.

Browser compatibility

Whether your browser can create the required WebGL context and execute the shader.

How to test a video card online

  1. Open the benchmark in a current browser.
  2. Use Standard and a 30-second run for your first comparison.
  3. Keep other GPU-heavy applications closed.
  4. Record the average FPS and 1% low, not just the live FPS number.
  5. Repeat the test if the result looks inconsistent.

Common reasons a graphics test score changes

Display synchronization: a refresh-rate ceiling can flatten the FPS number.
Thermal throttling: heat can lower clocks during sustained runs.
Power mode: laptops and mobile devices may limit GPU performance on battery.
Browser differences: graphics drivers and browser scheduling can affect WebGL performance.
