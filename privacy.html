Privacy policy

Short version: the benchmark runs in your browser and this site does not collect your results.

Data flow

Your browser runs WebGL on your GPU, measures frame times, and shows results on the page. Results are not sent anywhere by this site.

What the page reads

To show test details, the page reads your GPU renderer name, WebGL version, screen size, pixel ratio and browser name. This stays in your browser unless you copy or download a result yourself.

Cookies, analytics and third parties

The site currently sets no cookies, runs no analytics, loads no third-party scripts, fonts or CDN files, and has no error monitoring or shared-result storage.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard server logs such as IP addresses under its own privacy policy, outside this site's control.

Changes

If analytics, ads or shared results are added later, this page will be updated before they go live.

Contact

Report privacy questions through the repository's Issues page.
