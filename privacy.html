Privacy at Volume Shader BM

Last updated October 8, 2026. This page explains what the browser benchmark needs and what changes when advertising or analytics are enabled.

Benchmark results stay local

The benchmark itself calculates FPS and frame-time metrics in the browser. Local history can be stored using browser storage so you can revisit your own runs. A server-side results database is not required for the benchmark to work.

Website requests

To use the site, your browser must request the page files, CSS, JavaScript and other assets from the website host. Your hosting provider may maintain standard technical logs needed to operate the service.

Advertising

The downloadable site package includes labeled ad placeholders but does not include a publisher-specific advertising account or network ID. When you add an advertising provider, that provider may process information such as cookies, approximate location, device/browser signals or advertising identifiers according to its own policies and applicable consent requirements.

Do not place personalized-ad or analytics scripts on the site without reviewing the provider's terms, consent requirements and your jurisdictional obligations.

Analytics

No analytics provider is required for the benchmark to operate. If you later add analytics, update this page with the provider, purpose, retention and user choices.

Changes

Update this policy when you materially change the data collected, add advertising, add analytics or introduce server-side result storage.
