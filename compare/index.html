How Were These Baseline Numbers Derived?

All baseline scores are recorded under Google Chrome (version 120+) with WebGL 2.0 active, hardware acceleration enabled, and full AC power connected. Mobile devices are tested on cold runs before thermal throttling activates. To submit your own hardware results or inspect your WebGL extensions, use our Hardware Inspector.
