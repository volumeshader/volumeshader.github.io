Phone GPU test

You can check how well your phone handles 3D graphics without installing anything. Open the page, tap start, and read the numbers.

Steps for a fair phone result

  1. Plug the phone in and turn off battery saver.
  2. Close other apps and keep the screen on.
  3. Begin with the Simple complexity and the Quick test.
  4. If it runs above 30 FPS, try Standard, then Advanced.
  5. Rest the phone for a minute between runs.

What scores to expect

Treat these as rough ranges, not promises. A recent flagship usually holds a high FPS even at Advanced. A mid-range phone often does well on Simple and Standard. An older or budget phone may stay under 30 FPS on Standard, which is normal for its class.

Why phone scores change

  • Heat: phones throttle fast, so a second run is often slower.
  • Power mode: battery saver can cut GPU speed.
  • Browser: Chrome, Safari and others handle WebGL differently.
  • Refresh rate: a 60 Hz screen caps FPS at 60 even if the GPU could do more.

Using it when buying a phone

Run the same level on each phone in the shop, under the same conditions. A low result is one clue, not proof that a phone is fake or faulty. Check the specs and reviews as well.
