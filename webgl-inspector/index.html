What this page shows you

This page asks your browser what graphics hardware it can use for WebGL. It then shows the answers in a table. Here is what the main items mean.

  • GPU renderer and vendor. The name of the graphics chip and its maker, as the browser reports them. This is the quickest way to check that the browser is using the GPU you expect.
  • WebGL API level. WebGL 2 is newer and more capable than WebGL 1. Most modern browsers support both.
  • WebGPU availability. Shows whether the browser offers the newer WebGPU interface.
  • Max texture size. The largest picture the GPU can use as a texture.
  • Max renderbuffer and viewport size. The largest off screen surface and the largest drawing area. These limit the biggest resolution you can render.
  • Uniform and varying vectors. How much constant data and passed data a shader may use. Complex shaders need enough of both.
  • Extensions. Optional features that the driver offers on top of the basic WebGL set.

How to use it when something goes wrong

  1. Check the renderer name. If it mentions software rendering, fix hardware acceleration first.
  2. On a laptop with two GPUs, check that the renderer is the dedicated card if you expect it to be.
  3. If a test shows a black canvas, check that the WebGL level is not empty and that the limits look normal.
  4. Press the copy button and keep the text. It helps when you ask for help or compare two devices.

For error messages and fixes, see the troubleshooting page. To test speed, open the benchmark. For a beginner guide, read how to test your GPU online.

Frequently asked questions

Why does the page show a general GPU name?+

Some browsers hide the exact GPU model for privacy and show a general name instead. That does not mean anything is wrong with your hardware.

What does a software renderer mean?+

If the name mentions SwiftShader, llvmpipe or software, the browser is drawing with the CPU and not the GPU. Turn on hardware acceleration and update your graphics driver.

What is WebGPU?+

WebGPU is a newer web graphics interface that is meant to replace WebGL over time. The page only checks whether your browser offers it. The Volume Shader BM test itself uses WebGL.

Is this page sending my hardware details anywhere?+

No. The values are read in your browser and shown on the page. The copy button puts a text summary on your clipboard, and you decide what to do with it.

Why does my phone show fewer extensions than a PC?+

The list depends on the GPU, the driver and the browser. Phones usually support a different set than desktop cards, and that is normal.
