WebGL Troubleshooting & Error Resolution

Diagnose and resolve common browser graphics issues, crash events, and hardware acceleration errors.

1. Error: "WebGL Context Lost"

Cause: Modern operating systems (Windows TDR, macOS WindowServer, Android SurfaceFlinger) monitor GPU responsiveness. If a single frame takes longer than 2.0 to 5.0 seconds to render on an overloaded GPU, the OS forces a driver reset and kills the browser's WebGL context to prevent system freezing.

Solution: Lower the canvas resolution from 1024×1024 to 512×512 using the resolution dropdown before initiating the test. On laptops with dual GPUs, ensure your high-performance discrete GPU (NVIDIA/AMD) is assigned to your browser in OS graphics settings.

2. Problem: Extremely Low FPS (1-3 FPS) on Capable Hardware

Cause: Your browser has fallen back to software emulation (Google SwiftShader or Mesa llvmpipe) because hardware acceleration is disabled or your graphics driver has been blacklisted due to outdated versions.

Solution: Open chrome://gpu in your browser address bar. Check if "WebGL" and "WebGL 2" report "Hardware accelerated". If they say "Software only", update your graphics card drivers, enable hardware acceleration in browser settings, and restart your browser.

3. Problem: Black Screen or Canvas Fails to Render

Cause: Fragment shader precision mismatch. Some older mobile GPU drivers do not support highp float precision in fragment shaders, causing compilation failure.

Solution: Use our Hardware Inspector to verify if highp float precision is supported by your driver. VolumeShader BM includes automatic precision fallbacks to ensure compatibility across older Android handsets.
