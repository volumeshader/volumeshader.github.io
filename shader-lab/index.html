How the Distance Estimator Function Works

The raymarching engine queries float kernal(vec3 ver) at points along the ray in 3D space. When the returned float value is positive (> 0.0), the ray has penetrated the interior of the solid volumetric surface. When negative, the ray remains in empty space.

The root-finding bisection solver then refines the intersection distance using the golden ratio constants (M_L = 0.381966 and M_R = 0.618033) to pinpoint the exact surface boundary with sub-pixel precision. Normal vectors are calculated via finite differences across nearby samples to apply specular lighting.
