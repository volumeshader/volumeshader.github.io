Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you use VolumeShader BM at volumeshader.github.io. We try to keep it short and clear.

1. The tests run on your device

The benchmark, stress test, diagnostics and Shader Lab run inside your browser, using your own GPU. We do not have accounts, and we do not upload your scores, your frame times or your shader edits to our own server. If you press the copy button or download a score card, that file stays with you.

2. Hardware information

To show the GPU name on screen, the page asks your browser for the renderer and vendor text. It is displayed to you. A score summary that you copy may include it. It is not sent anywhere by the test code.

3. Google Tag Manager and measurement

This site uses Google Tag Manager, a service from Google that loads measurement or marketing tags on a website. Depending on which tags are active, Google or other providers may collect information such as the pages you visit, the time you spend, your browser and device type, your approximate location based on your IP address, and information from cookies or similar technologies. We use this to understand how the site is used and to improve it.

You can limit this by using your browser's privacy settings, by blocking cookies, or by using a browser extension that blocks trackers. Google also offers an opt out add-on for its analytics products at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

4. Cookies and local storage

The site itself does not need cookies to run the tests. Tags loaded through Google Tag Manager may set cookies. You can delete cookies at any time in your browser.

5. Advertising

At the time of writing, this site does not show advertising. If we add ads in the future, we will update this page first and explain which companies are involved, what cookies they use and what choices you have.

6. Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address when you request a page. Their own privacy statement explains how.

7. Links to other sites

Some pages link to other websites, such as GitHub. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

8. Children

The site is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, such as the right to access or delete it. Because we do not run accounts, most data about you is held by the services listed above. You can contact us to ask a question.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the site changes. The date at the top shows the last update.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent by opening an issue on the site's GitHub repository.

VolumeShader BM

A free browser based GPU benchmark and stress test. The main test draws a 3D fractal with WebGL raymarching and shows live FPS, 1% low and a score. This is an independent site and is not run by the author of the original volumeshader_bm page.

The test runs on your own GPU and your results stay in your browser. See the privacy policy for how Google Tag Manager is used on this site.
