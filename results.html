How to compare results responsibly

Keep consistentWhy
Benchmark versionA changed workload can make old scores incomparable.
PresetEach level intentionally performs a different amount of shader work.
BrowserBrowser and driver behavior can affect WebGL frame timing.
Power and temperatureClock limits and thermal throttling change sustained results.
Display / resolutionSynchronization and pixel count can change observed FPS.

Results are not a universal GPU ranking

Use these numbers as repeatable observations under a defined workload. A single shader test cannot replace a full gaming, CPU, VRAM or professional application benchmark suite.
