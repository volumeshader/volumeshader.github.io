About VolumeShader BM

VolumeShader BM is a free website where you can test your graphics card or phone GPU in the browser. It is built for people who want a quick, honest answer to the question "how fast is my GPU, and does it stay fast when it gets hot?"

What you can do here

  • Run a live volume shader test with FPS, frame time and 1% low.
  • Run a 20 second benchmark and save a score card.
  • Check heat and throttling with the stress test.
  • See what your browser knows about your GPU on the diagnostics page.
  • Edit the formula in the Shader Lab.
  • Learn how it all works in the blog.

What we believe

  • The tool comes first. Every tool page works right away. There are no accounts, no paywalls and nothing to install.
  • Explain things simply. Our articles are written in plain language, so a beginner can follow them.
  • Say only what we can support. When something is an estimate, we say so. For example, the numbers on the comparison page are rough reference values and not lab results.
  • Be clear about the original. We credit the original idea and do not pretend to be the original page.

Our relationship with the original page

The original volumeshader_bm page is hosted by the developer cznull on GitHub Pages at github.com/cznull/cznull.github.io. This site is an independent project. It is not run by cznull and has no official link with that person. We use the same kind of Mandelbulb raymarching scene and add a live HUD, a timed benchmark, a stress test, diagnostics, a shader editor and guides. You can read more in cznull, cznull.github.io/vsbm and the original page.

How the test runs and what we collect

The benchmark runs on your own device, and your results stay in your browser unless you choose to copy or download them. The site uses Google Tag Manager to manage measurement tags, which may collect general usage information. The privacy policy explains this in detail.

Hosting

The site is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages at volumeshader.github.io. Static hosting keeps pages fast and simple.

Contact and corrections

If you find a mistake, want to suggest a topic, or are the author of something we mention and want a change, please open an issue on the site's GitHub repository. We read every message and fix errors quickly.

VolumeShader BM

A free browser based GPU benchmark and stress test. The main test draws a 3D fractal with WebGL raymarching and shows live FPS, 1% low and a score. This is an independent site and is not run by the author of the original volumeshader_bm page.

The test runs on your own GPU and your results stay in your browser. See the privacy policy for how Google Tag Manager is used on this site.
