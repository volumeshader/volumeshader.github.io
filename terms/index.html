2. Hardware and safety notice

The tests run heavy calculations on your graphics hardware. This raises power use and heat. Modern GPUs have protection that slows them down or resets the driver, but you run the tests at your own risk. Be extra careful with phones, tablets, thin laptops and any device with weak cooling, dust or blocked vents. Do not run long tests on a charging phone. Stop a test if the device becomes too hot to touch comfortably, makes unusual noises or shows broken graphics.

VolumeShader BM

A free browser based GPU benchmark and stress test. The main test draws a 3D fractal with WebGL raymarching and shows live FPS, 1% low and a score. This is an independent site and is not run by the author of the original volumeshader_bm page.

The test runs on your own GPU and your results stay in your browser. See the privacy policy for how Google Tag Manager is used on this site.
