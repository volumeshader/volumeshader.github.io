Hardware Thermal Notice

VolumeShader BM executes high-intensity floating-point loops directly on your graphics hardware. By using this website, you acknowledge that intense GPU testing increases power consumption and heat generation. While modern graphics processors contain built-in thermal safety mechanisms that throttle clocks or reset drivers to prevent physical harm, users assume full responsibility for running sustained stress tests on passively cooled or battery-powered devices.

No Warranty

The tools, benchmark algorithms, and baseline scores provided on this website are offered 'as is' for informational and diagnostic purposes without warranties of any kind.
