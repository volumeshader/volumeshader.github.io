What is a GPU shader?

A shader is a small program executed by the graphics pipeline. In this benchmark, the fragment shader is responsible for constructing a ray, sampling the volume, running the Mandelbulb-style function and accumulating color for each pixel.

What is a volumetric shader?

Instead of drawing only the surface of a polygon, a volume renderer samples data throughout a 3D region. Ray marching is a common way to do this: a ray passes through the volume and the shader repeatedly evaluates samples along that path.

ConceptWhy it matters here
Fragment shaderRuns per rendered pixel and contains most of the workload.
Ray marchingAdds repeated sampling along the view ray.
