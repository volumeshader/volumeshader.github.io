How to Identify GPU Thermal Throttling in Real Time

When a graphics card or mobile SoC encounters its temperature threshold (typically 80°C–90°C on desktop GPUs, or 42°C skin temperature on phones), the onboard Power Management Integrated Circuit (PMIC) steps down clock frequencies to prevent hardware damage.

  • Stable Cooling: Framerate stays within ±5% across 2 minutes of testing. The frametime waveform remains smooth and flat.
  • Moderate Throttling: Framerate gradually drops by 10%–20% after 30 seconds as cooling fans spin up to maximum speed.
  • Severe Throttling: Framerate drops by 40% or more, characterized by periodic frametime spikes exceeding 45ms (sawtooth graph).
